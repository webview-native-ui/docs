# Homepage app screenshots

The homepage shows completed app screens, not a before/after comparison with Ionic.
Both images are direct, unretouched browser captures of the public iOS 27 theme demo,
at a 390 × 844 CSS-pixel viewport on 2026-11-11.

- `projects/docs/public/assets/showcase/library.jpg`: https://ionic-theme-ios27.rdlabo.dev/main/album
  after the photo gallery has loaded. Photography is supplied by the existing demo's Picsum feed.
- `projects/docs/public/assets/showcase/now-playing.jpg`: https://ionic-theme-ios27.rdlabo.dev/main/index/native-ui-extension
  after choosing **Open Now Playing** and waiting for the modal transition and artwork.
  The artwork is the demo's existing `mini-player-artwork.svg`.

These are Web-rendered demo screens, not captures of SwiftUI controls, device Dynamic Color,
or a complete shipped music application. The visible caption identifies Ionic and the iOS 27 theme.
The component styles only frame the full images; they do not alter the captured UI.

To update, capture the real demo at the same viewport, wait for images and transitions,
then inspect the page at desktop and mobile widths. Keep the screen content readable,
and preserve the original screenshots rather than manufacturing native UI in an image editor.
